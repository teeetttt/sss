How to Play
  • Win the game by walking any piece onto the opponent's home row, or by leaving them with no legal moves.
  • Moving: step one square in any direction, jump over one of your own pieces in front or diagonally, or place a piece from your reserve hand onto the board.
  • Capturing follows classic Rock–Paper–Scissors: each piece beats one type and loses to another.
  • Captured pieces join your reserve hand — you can drop them back in on your own four rows.
  • Draws happen after threefold repetition, or 50 moves in a row with no capture or drop.
