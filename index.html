Rules & How to Play
  • Victory: Reach the opponent's 1st row (baseline) with any piece, or trap them with no legal moves!
  • Movement: Move 1 step in any direction. Jump over 1 friendly piece in front/diagonal or Place a piece from your reserve hand.
  • Capturing: Capture opponent pieces following classic Rock-Paper-Scissors rules.
  • Re-entry (Drop): Captured pieces go into your reserve hand to drop back onto your starting 4 rows!
